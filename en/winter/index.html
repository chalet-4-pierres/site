Winter at the chalet: skiing, snowshoeing and sledging

The chalet faces the small Grand Valtin ski area, 8 km from the Gérardmer ski resort.

Request to book Check availability

Right outside: Grand Valtin

The small Grand Valtin ski area is just across from the chalet. A drag lift serves easy slopes, well suited to beginners and children. Check its opening days before your stay. Snowshoe trails start close by.

Downhill skiing in Gérardmer, 8 km away

The La Mauselaine ski area has around twenty runs, from green to black, over about 40 km. It suits families and beginners well.

Cross-country skiing and snowshoeing

The cross-country trails of Gérardmer link up with those of Xonrupt-Longemer and La Bresse, for a total of about 100 km. You can snowshoe around the chalet and on the marked routes of the ski resorts.

Practical information

  • Winter tyres or snow chainsEssential when it snows.

  • The wood-burning stoveThe chalet is heated by a heat pump. When you come in from the snow, the stove adds a crackling fire in the living area: remember the firewood option.

  • The utility roomWith its tumble dryer, it is handy for drying ski gear.

  • By the weekDuring the French winter school holidays, the chalet is rented by the week.
