Summer at the chalet: lakes, hikes and the Route des Crêtes

In summer, the chalet is a base for the lakes, the forest and the ridges of the High Vosges. In the evening, you come back to the terrace, facing the valley.

Request to book Check availability

The lakes

  • Lake Longemer, 8 km awaySwimming, canoeing, paddleboarding.

  • Lake Gérardmer, 8 km awaySwimming, water sports, a walk around the lake.

  • Lake Retournemer, just past LongemerA small, quiet lake at the foot of the forests, for a stroll.

  • Lac Blanc, on the Alsace sideA mountain lake surrounded by cliffs, at the start of several hikes.

Hiking and mountain biking

  • The Chemin de Sérichamp, from the chaletA 4.5 km loop via the high pasture and the Chemin du Grand Boroge, about 150 m of ascent. An easy walk, no car needed.

  • Les Roches du Valtin, from Le Valtin6.2 km and about 390 m of ascent, with viewpoints over the valley.

  • The Belbriette valley, in Ban-sur-Meurthe-ClefcyAbout 10 km, moderate, allow 2½ hours.

  • The Sentier des Roches, from the Col de la SchluchtOne of the most difficult trails in the Vosges, for experienced hikers. Closed from 1 November to 30 April.

Mountain biking is possible on the marked trails in the area.

Distances and ascents are approximate. To find more hikes around the chalet and be guided on the spot, download the Cirkwi app.

By car

The Route des Crêtes, the scenic road along the Vosges ridges, passes close by, with the Hohneck summit and the Col de la Schlucht pass. Alsace and its Wine Route are within reach for a day trip.

At the chalet

  • Pétanque on the chalet grounds
  • Meals on the terrace, facing the valley
  • An afternoon nap in the fresh air, in peace and quiet

Practical information

In July and August, as during the French winter school holidays, at Christmas and at New Year, the chalet is rented by the week. The rest of the year, it can be rented from 2 nights.
