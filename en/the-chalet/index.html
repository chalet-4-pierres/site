The chalet, floor by floor

The chalet covers 240 m² over 3 floors. Each floor has its own bedrooms, shower room and toilet. Families or groups can spread out without getting in each other's way, and everyone keeps a quiet corner.

An eco-friendly chalet

The chalet is built from solid wood and insulated with cellulose wadding. An earth-to-air heat exchanger (a "Canadian well") draws outside air through underground pipes before it enters the house: it cools the air in summer and pre-warms it in winter. Heating comes from an air-source heat pump and the wood-burning stove.

The chalet runs on a septic tank. Please use gentle cleaning products, without bleach.
