Getting to the chalet

The chalet is in Grand Valtin, a hamlet of Ban-sur-Meurthe-Clefcy, 8 km from Gérardmer, in the Vosges mountains of eastern France.

Address

Easiest: search for “Chalet Les 4 Pierres” in Google Maps or Waze.

Chemin de Sérichamp, hameau du Grand Valtin, 88230 Ban-sur-Meurthe-Clefcy, Vosges, France
GPS: 48° 05' 47'' N, 6° 59' 39'' E

Open in Google Maps

By car, from Gérardmer

  1. Head towards Xonrupt-Longemer.
  2. Turn left towards Le Valtin.
  3. Drive through the hamlet of Grand Valtin.
  4. Just before leaving the hamlet, turn left into the Chemin de Sérichamp, opposite the ski slope.

By train

The nearest station is Saint-Dié-des-Vosges, 30 minutes from the chalet by car. Plan on a hire car for your stay.

In winter

The chalet is at 1,000 m altitude. When it snows, winter tyres or snow chains are essential.

For shopping: the nearest bakery is in Xonrupt-Longemer, 6 km away, and the supermarkets are in Gérardmer.

Eat just down the path

The Auberge du Grand Valtin is right below the chalet, within walking distance. Stéphanie and Alain serve daily specials and regional set menus in a warm, family atmosphere: tarte flambée, spätzle, mountain specialities. We recommend booking ahead.

For breakfast, the inn bakes bread and croissants on site, to order.
