How it works

  1. You send your request using this form.
  2. We confirm the dates and the price of your stay, and tell you how to pay the deposit.
  3. Your booking is confirmed as soon as we receive the €250 deposit.
  4. You pay the balance by bank transfer 15 days before arrival, or on site in cash or by cheque.

Good to know

The chalet can also be booked on Booking.com, Abritel (Vrbo) and Airbnb. Booking direct, you deal with us without a middleman, and without the platforms' service fees.
