Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before booking the chalet for 14 in Grand Valtin.

The chalet

How many people does the chalet sleep?
14 people, in 6 bedrooms: 4 double bedrooms with a 140 cm bed and 2 children's bedrooms with six 90 cm beds in bunk beds.
Is there a bathroom on every floor?
Yes. Each of the 3 floors has its own shower room and toilet.
Are there bedrooms on the ground floor?
Yes. The ground floor has a double bedroom and a children's bedroom, with their own shower room.
Is the chalet accessible for guests with reduced mobility?
It is not certified as accessible and does not have all the equipment of an adapted home. But the first floor, the main level, can be reached from outside without stairs. It has the living area, 2 double bedrooms and a shower room with a large walk-in shower, and easy access to the terrace.
Does the chalet have Wi-Fi?
Yes.
Are pets allowed?
Yes, at no extra charge. We simply ask you to make sure they treat the chalet with care.
Is there equipment for babies?
Yes. A baby cot and a high chair are available. Ask for them when you book.
Is there a raclette machine?
No. Feel free to bring your own.
Is there a swimming pool or hot tub?
No.

Linen and cleaning

Is bed linen provided?
As an optional extra, at €12 per bed. You can also bring your own.
Are towels provided?
No. Please bring your bath towels and tea towels.
Is cleaning included?
End-of-stay cleaning is an optional extra, at €90. Otherwise, the chalet must be left clean. Please use gentle products, without bleach: the chalet has a septic tank.

Your stay

What are the arrival and departure times?
Arrival from 4 pm, departure by 10 am. Let us know your arrival time a few days before.
Can I rent the chalet for a weekend?
Yes, except during the French winter and summer school holidays, at Christmas and at New Year: €700 for the Saturday night, €200 for the 2nd night (Friday or Sunday). During those periods, the chalet is rented by the week, Saturday to Saturday.
Is the tourist tax included?
Yes, it is included in all rates.
Is there firewood for the stove?
Yes, as an optional extra at €50.
Where can I buy groceries?
The Auberge du Grand Valtin, just below the chalet, bakes bread and croissants on site, to order. The nearest bakery is in Xonrupt-Longemer, 6 km away, and the supermarkets are in Gérardmer, 8 km away.
Is there a restaurant nearby?
Yes. The Auberge du Grand Valtin, run by Stéphanie and Alain, is just below the chalet, within walking distance. It serves daily specials and regional set menus. Remember to book ahead.
Do I need snow equipment for the car?
Yes, when it snows: winter tyres or snow chains. The chalet is in the mountains, in the High Vosges.

Booking and payment

How do I book direct?
Send a request using the form. We confirm the dates and price by email, and the booking is confirmed once we receive a deposit of €250.
Which payment methods do you accept?
Bank transfer, cheque and cash. French holiday vouchers (chèques-vacances) are not accepted.
What happens if I cancel?
More than 15 days before arrival: free of charge. Less than 15 days before: the €250 deposit is kept.
Is the chalet on Booking.com, Abritel (Vrbo) or Airbnb?
Yes. You can also book direct on this website, without the platforms' service fees.

Book direct

Book direct with us, with no booking platform fees. Check the free dates, then send your request. We reply within 48 hours with a confirmation and how to pay the deposit.
