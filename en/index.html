A large eco-friendly chalet for 14 people, in the heart of the Vosges

Le Chalet des Quatre Pierres is a 240 m² chalet rented in full, for families and groups of friends. It stands in Grand Valtin, a hamlet at 1,000 m altitude, 8 km from Gérardmer in eastern France. It sleeps 14 people in 6 bedrooms, over 3 floors.

Highlights

  • An eco-friendly chalet.Solid wood, cellulose insulation, an earth-to-air heat exchanger.

  • A shower room and toilet on every floor.Each family has its own space, ground floor included.

  • A large living area.A table for 14, a wood-burning stove and 3 picture windows over the valley.

  • Opposite the Grand Valtin ski slope.Children learn to ski right across from the chalet.

  • Pets are welcome.

Around the chalet

In winter, downhill skiing in Gérardmer, cross-country skiing and snowshoeing. In summer, the lakes of Longemer and Gérardmer, hiking and the Route des Crêtes, the scenic road along the Vosges ridges.

Eat just down the path

Right below the chalet, the Auberge du Grand Valtin, run by Stéphanie and Alain, serves daily specials and regional set menus in a warm, family atmosphere. You can walk there. Remember to book a table.
